IPEDS custom comparison groups

Every year, colleges can tell NCES which other schools they want to be compared with in their IPEDS Data Feedback Report. These pages draw those choices as networks: an arrow from one school to another means the first listed the second, and red lines mark pairs that listed each other.

2024 network

Comparison groups from the IPEDS 2024-25 release (CUSTOMCGIDS2024), limited to schools that offer a bachelor's degree. Includes a school search.

The page downloads the data straight from NCES. The first visit takes a few minutes; after that your browser keeps a copy and it opens almost instantly.
