- 315 have a closing date on record; 134 were merged into another institution.
- 399 were last in the directory in 2018 or earlier, so the groups attributed to them are at least several years old.
- 378 last reported a bachelor's degree or higher as their highest award. That is an approximation: a graduate-only school also reports a high award without offering a bachelor's.
